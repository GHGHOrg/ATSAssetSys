Markers

  1. Header, always visible: portfolio name, value, shares, cost, "Unrealized gain/loss" (4.8) and the price age line; a stale price or "no price, at cost" shows its flag, as on view 3. It is one holding, a ticker within one portfolio. A back arrow returns to where you came from.
  2. Buy and Sell open the entry with the ticker already filled in; Split starts a split entry for this ticker and portfolio (navigation outline). Whether the buttons are offered at 0 shares is left to the entry rules (4.4).
  3. Three tabs, left to right: Transactions, Lots, Classification. It opens on Transactions (the first tab); the tab is not saved.
  4. Transactions tab: this holding's buys, sells and splits, newest first, each with type, date and "ET", quantity and amount. A reversal shows as such and links to its original. Tapping a row opens the transaction, where Reverse and Reverse and re-enter start (4.6).
  5. Lots tab: each lot that still has shares, oldest first, with purchase date and "ET", quantity left, cost per share, lot cost and unrealized gain/loss (green or red with sign, "no price" instead when unpriced). Quantities and cost per share include splits recorded so far (4.4). Sold-out lots are not listed; they show in Transactions.
  6. Classification tab: effective sector and geography, each labelled "From the source", "Override" or "Unclassified" (4.9). Edit picks a value from the matching list (dialogs are drawn with the entry flows, wireframe 10). Clear override returns to the source value, or "Unclassified".
  7. Split history: every split recorded for this holding, so a forgotten split is easier to spot (C9). With none, one plain line.
  8. Format only: a split row, shown as date and ratio (2 for 1).
  9. Hide button, shown only at 0 shares, no confirmation (4.4). Unhide is in Hidden holdings (view 9).
  10. A holding with no shares shows one plain line on the Lots tab. Its Transactions tab still lists everything.

Reached from a portfolio's holdings list, from Transaction history and from Hidden holdings. Realized gains for this holding are on view 5, not here.
