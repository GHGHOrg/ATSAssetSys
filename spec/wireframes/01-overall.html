Markers

  1. Backup reminder banner. Shown only when due (older than 7 days, or never backed up and the app holds data). The button starts the export. Dismiss hides it until the app is next opened (section 9).
  2. Net worth figure, with the age of the prices, or an "updating" indicator while a refresh runs. Tapping it opens Net Worth (Total).
  3. Allocation of net worth, with an "Allocation by" dropdown: Asset type (default), Portfolio, Holding, Sector, Geography. The choice is not saved, so Overall starts on Asset type each time the app is opened or unlocked. Drawn as a donut with a small gap between slices. A slice of 5% or more shows its percentage on the donut. A row in the list shows its percentage only for a slice under 5% (a 0.0% slice has no arc). Each row has a swatch, a name and an amount, and its TalkBack label reads the percentage in full. Cash is its own slice in every option. Asset type always lists cash, stocks and ETFs, even at 0.0%. The other options list only what has value. Tapping the donut or the list opens Net Worth (Total); the dropdown does not.
  4. Each portfolio with its value and gain/loss (green with a plus sign for a gain, red with a minus sign for a loss). Tapping a row opens that portfolio's holdings.
  5. One quiet line, shown only when a price refresh failed. No pop-up.
  6. Buy/Sell button. It opens the short menu Buy, Sell.
  7. Guided steps when the app holds no data. Only step 1 is active; steps 2 and 3 are shown but locked.
  8. Restore is offered only in More, not here.
