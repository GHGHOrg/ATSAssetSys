Markers

  1. About is the fifth entry in More, after Backup and restore (CR5). The other entries and the gear for Settings are unchanged (wireframe 10f).
  2. The app's name as the phone shows it, and its version as the app reports it: versionName (x.y.z) in your app. One scrolling screen with a back arrow that returns to More. TalkBack reads "ATS Asset System, version 1.1.0". The version shown here is only an example; it is whatever the installed build says.
  3. "Open-source licences": one row per library the app ships, with its name and its licence's name, in a fixed order. Each row is a drop-down that opens in place: tapping it shows that licence's full text under the row, tapping again closes it (▾ closed, ▴ open; try it here). All rows start closed each time About opens, and more than one can be open. Rows are at least 48 dp high, and TalkBack reads each as expandable or collapsible. The text opens inside the screen, not as a pop-up menu, which would take MainActivity's window focus (CLAUDE.md section 6, the cover-screen pitfall). Nothing on this screen is sent, copied or shared, and it needs no network.
  4. Open rows, two drawn open for the example (kotlinx.coroutines and SQLCipher for Android), to show that more than one can be open: the licence text in one block under its row, taken from the library in Build; the screen scrolls as one.
  5. The development app (AC-65) shows its own name and x.y.z-dev.n, n its versionCode (CLAUDE.md section 4). The list is the same.
