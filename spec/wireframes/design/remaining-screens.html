Remaining screens

The pieces the catalog left out, drawn in the decided system (D-07 to D-17) from what the app does today: the Sell screen's lot picker (SellScreen.kt, wireframe 10a), an entry screen with the keyboard up at 200% font, the date field, and the lock and cover screens. ATS has no date picker today; D-18 adds one through intent-cr-07, so every date field here carries the calendar icon that opens it, and typing (YYYY-MM-DD) still works. The pickers themselves are in the Component Catalog. Icons are Lucide at its own stroke of 2 (D-19, D-24, D-25). Made-up test data; every text is a placeholder (D9), taken from wireframe 10a where it has one. Drawn in HTML at 1 px per dp, not rendered by Android.
