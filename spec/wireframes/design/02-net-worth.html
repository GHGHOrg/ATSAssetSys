Choices made in this drawing, for you to confirm. Everything else follows D-37 and D-38 (the summary card, the donut and its rows).

  1. The summary card has no chevron here: on Overall it opens this screen; here it opens nothing.
  2. Composition is the review card's label-and-value rows (D-31) with "Net worth" as the total line, under a medium 16 heading inside the card.
  3. Each breakdown is its own card, headed in medium 16 inside the card ("By asset type"), then Overall's donut and rows. On Overall the dropdown picks one; here all five are stacked, so the heading replaces the dropdown.
  4. Rows on this screen are read-only: 48 tall for the large font, with no chevron, since nothing opens from them.
  5. Net worth of $0.00 is one plain line in a card in secondary 16, in place of everything under the summary card.

Not drawn: the updating and failed-refresh lines (as on Overall, frame C), 200% font. Icons are Lucide at stroke 2, drawn inline from its path data as Claude knows it.
