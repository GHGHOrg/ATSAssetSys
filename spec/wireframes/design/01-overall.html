Choices made in this drawing, for you to confirm. Everything else follows D-13, D-17, D-30 to D-36, and the slice colors (D-07, unchanged).

  1. The backup reminder is the catalog's card with actions at the top: the message in 16, Back up now gold, Dismiss outline. Not the notice banner (D-33): it asks for an action.
  2. Net worth is the summary card: label, the 32 figure, Investments in 16, the 1-day line indented in condensed 12 in the summary card's own gain color, and the price age in condensed 12. A chevron at its top right shows it opens Net Worth (Total).
  3. "Allocation by" is the dense pick field (D-30) in the allocation card's head, with its label beside it.
  4. The donut is 168 across with a 30 ring and a small gap between slices; slices use the existing slice colors, cash its own; a percentage on a slice of 5% or more in Asap Condensed 12 medium, white in light, black in dark (slice-label).
  5. Allocation rows are 48 tall with a 16 round swatch, the name and the amount in 16; a slice under 5% shows its percentage in secondary 14 after the amount.
  6. Portfolios get a section heading (as Settings, D-36) over the catalog's tappable rows: value 16, gain with its percentage in 14, the 1-day line in condensed 12, chevron.
  7. A failed refresh is one quiet line in secondary 14 under the net worth card, with no icon; updating shows loader-circle beside the age line.
  8. Buy/Sell stays an outline button at the bottom right, as on Cash (D-30).
  9. Locked guided steps use the disabled state (38%) with Lucide's lock icon and the word "locked", so the meaning is never the fading alone.

Not drawn: the dropdown open (the dense pick field's list, inside the window), the other "Allocation by" choices, these at 200% font. Icons are Lucide at stroke 2, drawn inline from its path data as Claude knows it.
