Choices made in this drawing, for you to confirm. Everything else follows D-17, D-31, D-32 and D-33.

  1. Every dialog is the catalog's dialog: title in 22, content, actions at the bottom right with Cancel as a text button and the one main action filled gold, medium 40 (not the large full-width foot of entry screens).
  2. A dialog's guidance is the field's note line (the name rules, "Only the name changes", "Delete is available when the name matches").
  3. All three name refusals are shown on the field (bronze outline, circle-alert, the message in place of the note), the duplicate name included, since the wireframe puts them all under the field. No blocked card in these dialogs.
  4. The delete summary is label-and-value rows straight on the dialog (no card inside it, which wouldn't show on the raised surface in light). The cash balance is two rows, "now" and "after", the after as the total line, instead of "$9,600.00 → $10,000.00" on one line, so it never wraps at large fonts. The realized gain is colored (spec 6).
  5. The "cannot be undone" warning is a line in medium 14 with Lucide's triangle-alert in the icon color. Not bronze: nothing is wrong yet.
  6. Delete portfolio is gold filled, the dialog's main action; the typed name is the guard. Red stays for losses, bronze for errors, so there is no "danger" color.
  7. Delete unavailable is the catalog's disabled button (38%, solid), not the wireframe's dashed outline (as D-29 for chips); for intent-cr-07's list of departures. TalkBack reads it as unavailable; the note says why.
  8. A blocked deletion is a dialog titled "Can't delete …" with D-31's blocked card inside, the fix as plain text, and Close as the one filled button.

Not drawn: the dialogs at 200% font with the keyboard up (the dialog then scrolls inside and stays above the keyboard, CLAUDE.md pitfall), and the ⋮ menu open (the catalog's). Icons are Lucide at stroke 2, drawn inline from its path data as Claude knows it.
