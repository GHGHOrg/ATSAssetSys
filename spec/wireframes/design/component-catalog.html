Component Catalog

Component catalog

Each component of the Corporate Design System drawn once with its states, light on the left and dark on the right, from the decided tokens: navy and gold (D-07, D-10), cards with shadow and no border (D-08), Asap and Asap Condensed (D-12) on the figure-led scale (D-13), radius 8 (D-14) and tight spacing (D-15). Made-up test data; every label is a placeholder, since wording is decided on the phone (D9). Drawn in HTML at 1 px per dp, not rendered by Android.

Buttons

Radius 8, 40 tall with a 48 touch area, Asap 14 medium. Gold fills only the one main action on a screen.

Button sizes

Three sizes, each in filled, outline and text, all radius 8. Large 48 tall, Asap 16 medium, padding 24. Medium 40 tall, Asap 14 medium, padding 20 (the default). Small 32 tall, Asap Condensed 14 medium, padding 12; its touch area is still 48 (spec 6), so small buttons need 8 dp of free space around them. Labels are placeholders (D9).

Text fields

Two sizes. Standard: outlined, radius 8, 56 tall, Asap 16, its label riding on the outline. Dense: 40 tall inside a 48 touch area, Asap 14, padding 8, no label of its own: its label sits beside it in the row, and a refusal's message sits right-aligned under it. Focus thickens the outline to 2 (navy 10 in light, gold in dark); a refusal uses bronze (D-16). Field outlines: 4.94:1 light, 5.17:1 dark.

Rows

Two densities. Standard: padding 10, body 16, as many lines as the row needs. Dense: one line, Asap 14 with Asap Condensed 12 for a secondary figure, padding 4 above and below; 48 tall when tappable, 36 when read-only. Neither goes under 48 when tappable. Pressed adds a 10% layer of ink.

Checkbox

Two sizes: standard (18 dp box, 2 dp outline, label Asap 16) and dense (16 dp box, 1.5 dp outline, label Asap 14). Both rows stay at least 48 tall. The box has corner 4, the one exception to radius 8 (D-14): at this size 8 would make it look like a radio button. Unchecked: a 2 dp outline in button-line (4.55:1 on the light card). Checked: filled navy 9 with a white check (light), gold with a navy check (dark). Used by the lot picker; its label sits beside it and the whole row toggles it.

Radio button

Added with Settings (D-36). A 20 dp circle with a 2 dp outline in button-line; selected turns the outline to the check color and adds a 10 dp dot (navy 9 in light, gold in dark, as the checkbox). The row is the touch target, at least 48 tall, label Asap 16; the default choice tagged in Asap Condensed 12. A choice that only saves applies on tap; one with side effects confirms with a gold button. TalkBack reads each as selected or not selected.

Icon button

Added with tab order (D-36). 48 dp square, a 24 dp Lucide icon in the button color (in the band, the band's text color), no outline, radius 8 for the pressed layer. Always a TalkBack label that names the action, so the meaning never rests on the picture. Disabled at 38%.

Chips

Selected: navy 9 with white (14.43:1) in light, gold with navy 10 (9.44:1) in dark.

Tabs inside a screen

Bottom bar

Three versions: text only (D-10), icon and text (ATS's choice since D-37, amending D-10), icon only. Icons are Lucide (D-19, D-25) at 24 dp with Lucide's own stroke of 2 (D-24), in the label color (navy 2, 7.18:1 on the bar; gold when selected): pie-chart, briefcase, wallet, more-horizontal.

Band and cards

Dialog

Light: the card tint; dark: navy 8 (#153D5A), so it floats above the page and cards. Dark text on it: 9.57:1, gold 6.21:1.

Date picker

A calendar layer drawn inside the window over a scrim, never Material's own picker window (which takes the window focus and fights the cover screen; CLAUDE.md pitfall). Opened from a calendar icon at the end of the date field; typing stays. The month name and day numbers are Asap, weekday letters Asap Condensed, Sunday first as in the US. Two sizes: standard (day cells 40, a Cancel and a confirm button) and dense (day cells 28, one-letter weekdays, the note left at the field, no buttons: a tap on a day picks and closes, the scrim or ✕ cancels). In both, days sit 48 apart each way, the touch minimum (spec 6), so the dense picker is smaller only around its grid. They pair with the fields: the standard date field opens the standard picker, the dense date field opens the dense picker, never crossed. ATS has no picker today: adding one there is a change request. Labels are placeholders (D9).

Entry and review parts

Added with the cash entry, split, detail and reversal screens (D-31; Cash Entry and Split page).

Menu

Notes

Errors in bronze brown (D-16). A refused field shows a 2 dp outline, Lucide's circle-alert (D-27) and its message in medium weight, all in bronze brown: #7A541F light (5.42:1), #E2BF8D dark (8.29:1). Red stays for losses; the mark and the message carry the meaning with the color.

Disabled is 38% opacity, as Material does; contrast rules don't apply to disabled controls. Pressed is a 10% layer of ink in light and of white in dark.

Elsewhere: the lot picker, the lock screen and the keyboard over an entry screen are on the Remaining Screens page. Icons are drawn inline from Lucide's path data as Claude knows it, not copied from a release, so a detail may differ slightly (redrawn 2026-11-27: Lucide's library from unpkg did not load in the user's view).
