Choices made in this drawing, for you to confirm. Everything else follows D-17, D-31 to D-34.

  1. The wizard's step is a line under the band in Asap Condensed 14 medium, secondary: "Step 1 of 5 · Nothing is changed until step 5." No progress bar.
  2. The Backup and restore screen is two cards, each with its own action: Back up now gold filled (the screen's main action), Restore from backup file an outline button.
  3. Restore offline is the disabled button (D-34) with the reason as a note line, not the wireframe's dashed outline; a third departure for intent-cr-07 (wireframe 10e, marker 4).
  4. An export's result is D-33's notice banner, both "Backup saved" and "Backup not saved": a cancelled export is not an error, so not bronze.
  5. Checking shows each phase as a 48 row: Lucide check when done, loader-circle turning while it runs (standing still under reduced motion), and the state in words.
  6. The error report is the blocked card with the count, then the errors in a card, one row per error with dividers, its line and column in medium, plain text.
  7. Now and In the file are two right-aligned columns with condensed heads; the after-restore figures are label-and-value rows under a divider.
  8. The wizard's foot is the entry screens' foot: the step's main action large gold, Cancel a large text button under it; Replace all data is gold too, disabled until RESTORE matches (as D-34).
  9. Restore complete gets Lucide's circle-check in the icon color beside its heading (not green: green is gains), the counts as three rows, and no back arrow.

Not drawn: these at 200% font with the keyboard up (RestoreScreen already measures its room, CLAUDE.md pitfall), a file-level failure (same layout, "File" in place of a line), and step 4 with RESTORE typed. Icons are Lucide at stroke 2, drawn inline from its path data as Claude knows it.
