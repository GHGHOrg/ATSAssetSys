Choices made in this drawing, for you to confirm. Everything else follows D-31 and D-38 to D-42.

  1. The header is the summary card: the portfolio's name as its label, the value in 32, Shares, Cost and Unrealized gain/loss in 16, the 1-day line and the price age. It stays fixed only while it takes at most half the screen (AC-61 (a)).
  2. Buy, Sell and Split are three equal outline buttons in one row under the header (medium 40); none is the screen's one main action.
  3. The tabs are the catalog's tabs inside a screen: Asap Condensed 14 medium, the selected one in the button color with a 3 dp line under it.
  4. A lot row: the date in medium 16, shares left with cost per share and the lot's cost in secondary 14 under it; value in 16 and gain in 14 on the right; no chevron (a lot opens nothing).
  5. A transaction row: type and date in regular 16 (as on Cash and Transaction history, D-44), shares in secondary 14, amount in 16, uncolored, and a chevron (it opens the transaction).
  6. Classification is label-and-value rows, each value with its source ("From the source", "Override", "Unclassified") in Asap Condensed 12 secondary under it; Edit and Clear override small text buttons, as on the list screens (D-36).
  7. Split history is a card of its own with a medium 16 heading; a split as its date and ratio; with none, one plain line.
  8. Hide is an outline button with Lucide's eye-off, under the header, only at 0 shares.

Not drawn: the header scrolling with the list at the largest font (more than half the screen), stale and "no price" flags (as on Portfolios), 200% font. Icons are Lucide at stroke 2, drawn inline from its path data as Claude knows it.
