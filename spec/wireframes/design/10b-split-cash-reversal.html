Choices made in this drawing, for you to confirm.

  1. One-at-a-time choices are chips (type, direction), the catalog's filter chip with its check. The catalog has no radio buttons or segmented control.
  2. Review, and Confirm and save, are the large full-width gold button at the foot, as on the Sell screen (D-17). Back to edit is a large outline button under it; on the reversal, Cancel is a text button.
  3. The amount field shows a "$" before the number, in the secondary text color, and is typed without it.
  4. Review and detail values are label-and-value rows in a card, label in secondary text; the result (cash effect, shares at this point) is a total line under a divider, in medium weight.
  5. Links to other entries are tappable rows with a chevron (the cash line, "Reversed on…", "Reversal of…"), not text links.
  6. Portfolio and ticker on the split are standard pick fields: 56 tall like the text field, with a 24 chevron-down. A new size of D-30's pick field.
  7. The split ratio is two small dense fields, at least 64 wide and sized to their content (as D-28), with "for" between.
  8. A blocked save is one card at the top, 2 dp bronze outline, circle-alert, "Not saved" in medium bronze, the reason in normal text. A field's own rule (note required, amount 0) shows on the field instead, as D-16.
  9. The selected tab is where the flow opened: Cash for cash entries, Portfolios for split, transaction detail and the reversal, Overall for the first-launch opening balance.

Not drawn: these screens at 200% font with the keyboard up (as Remaining Screens did for Sell), the pick lists open, and the other detail states as full screens. Icons are Lucide at stroke 2, drawn inline from its path data as Claude knows it.
