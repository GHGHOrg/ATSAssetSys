Choices made in this drawing, for you to confirm. Everything else follows D-17, D-18, D-31 and D-32.

  1. The replacing banner is a card with Lucide's info icon in the icon color, at the top of the entry screen. Not bronze: bronze stays for errors only (D-16).
  2. The original value of a changed field shows under it as the field's note line (regular 12, secondary), like the date's time-zone note; only on fields that differ from the original.
  3. The combined review is two cards, each headed in medium 16 ("1. Reverse the original", "2. Enter the replacement") and each ending in its own cash effect total line.
  4. The combined cash effect is a third card with one total line, signed and uncolored (marker 7), followed by "Both are saved together, or neither is." in secondary 14.
  5. Change is a small outline button in the reversal date's row, as Sell all (D-32, 3); the row is 48 tall.
  6. "Realized gain removed" stays uncolored; only the new realized gain in part 2 is green or red, so each review keeps one colored figure.
  7. A long review value goes under its label, full width (the reversal's prefilled note), instead of wrapping on the right.
  8. The sell's lot heading reads "Lots, as they will be after the reversal" in place of "Lots, oldest first", with the original's picks ticked (marker 9; wording D9).

Not drawn: these at 200% font with the keyboard up, the split's pair (as frame E, with ratios), and Change opened (it is 10b's reversal date field, frame I). Icons are Lucide at stroke 2, drawn inline from its path data as Claude knows it.
