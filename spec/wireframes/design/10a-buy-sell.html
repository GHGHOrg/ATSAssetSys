Choices made in this drawing, for you to confirm. Everything else follows D-17, D-18 and D-31.

  1. The derived price per share is a plain line under the quantity and total, in secondary text, not a field.
  2. Quantity and total sit side by side, as in the wireframe; the total's label is shortened to "Total, fees included" so it fits on the outline at half width (wording D9).
  3. Sell all is a small outline button (32, with its 48 touch area) on the price line.
  4. A stale price's age is condensed 12 under the gain, in secondary text; "no price" replaces the gain in secondary text.
  5. The pick mismatch uses the blocked card with "Not saved yet", at the top of the screen.
  6. In the sell review, "Lots used" is a sub-heading in medium secondary text inside the card; the realized gain is green or red with its sign (spec 6), the only colored figure.
  7. The Buy/Sell menu opens above its button, inside the window over a scrim, as the catalog's ⋮ menu.
  8. Nothing to enter shows as a card with the reason in medium and an outline button where one exists.

Not drawn: these at 200% font with the keyboard up (Remaining Screens has the Sell screen so), the pick lists open, and the sell review's lots at large fonts. Icons are Lucide at stroke 2, drawn inline from its path data as Claude knows it.
