Choices made in this drawing, for you to confirm. Everything else follows D-30 to D-39.

  1. A portfolio row is the catalog's tappable row with two lines on the left: the name in 16 and the holdings count in secondary 14; value, gain with its percentage and the 1-day line on the right; a chevron.
  2. New portfolio is an outline button with Lucide's plus, at the bottom left, opposite Buy/Sell; both outline, since neither is the screen's one main action.
  3. A portfolio's summary is the summary card, as net worth's: "Portfolio value" with the 32 figure, then Cost and Unrealized gain/loss as 16 lines, the 1-day line and the price age.
  4. The holdings card carries its own heading and column head: "Holdings" in medium 16 on the left, "Unrealized gain/loss" in Asap Condensed 14 medium, secondary, over the right column.
  5. A holding row's ticker is medium 16 (a portfolio name is regular), with its shares in secondary 14 under it.
  6. The three links are a card of 48 rows with a chevron (D-31's links), under the holdings.
  7. The ⋮ menu opens under its icon at the top right, as the catalog's menu over a scrim.

Not drawn: a fully sold holding at 0 shares, 200% font (C26's percentage on its own line). Icons are Lucide at stroke 2, drawn inline from its path data as Claude knows it.
