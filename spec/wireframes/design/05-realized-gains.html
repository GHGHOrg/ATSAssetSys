Choices made in this drawing, for you to confirm. Everything else follows D-28, D-31 and D-38 to D-40.

  1. From and To are standard date fields side by side with the calendar icon; the date note under them, and Clear dates as a small text button at the right of that line.
  2. The total is the summary card, its 32 figure in the summary card's own gain or loss color (#8FE08A / #FFC2BB), uncolored at zero; the count of sales and the range in condensed 12 under it.
  3. A holding row opens in place: chevron-down when closed, chevron-up when open; the gain in 16, colored, before it.
  4. A sale is a block inside the card, indented: its date and gain as a medium line, then quantity, proceeds and cost of lots used as D-31's label-and-value rows, then "Open in Transaction history" as a link row with a chevron.

Not drawn: the date picker open (Remaining Screens), a refused date, 200% font. Icons are Lucide at stroke 2, drawn inline from its path data as Claude knows it.
