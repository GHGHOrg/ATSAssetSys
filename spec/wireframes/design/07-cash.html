Choices made in this drawing, for you to confirm. (1) The balance sits on the navy summary card with the 32 figure, as on Overall and Holding detail (D-07, D-13); Cash entry is an outline button on it. (2) The ticker pick is a dense field with a chevron-down: a part the catalog doesn't have yet (it would open a list inside the window, like the ⋮ menu). (3) Unavailable chips are the catalog's disabled chip, 38% with a solid outline: decided (D-29), a departure from wireframe 07's dashed outline. (4) Rows are standard rows (two lines, tappable, chevron at the end); the balance after each entry is condensed 12. (5) Buy/Sell is an outline button, as the wireframe says, so this screen has no gold button. (6) Frame D, like the wireframe, leaves out the date fields.

Not drawn: the screen at 200% font and in landscape (the filter card is a fixed part only if it stays fixed, CLAUDE.md pitfall), the ticker list open, and the Cash entry form (the remaining entry screens come next).

Icons: Lucide at stroke 2, drawn inline from its path data as Claude knows it, so a detail may differ slightly.
