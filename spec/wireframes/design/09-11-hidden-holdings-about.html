Choices made in this drawing, for you to confirm. Everything else follows D-36 (More, list-screen actions), D-39 (empty line) and D-44 (portfolio line).

  1. A hidden holding's row: ticker in medium 16, "0 shares" and the last transaction in secondary 14, Unhide as a small text button with Lucide's eye under them (as Rename and Delete on list screens, D-36), and a chevron to Holding detail.
  2. About's head is a plain card: the app's name in 22 and the version in secondary 14, not the summary card (it holds no figure).
  3. A licence row is 48 tall: the library in 16, its licence in secondary 14 on the right, chevron-down closed and chevron-up open (as Realized gains, D-42).
  4. An open licence is a block under its row: Asap 14 regular on the page color inside the card, keeping the text's own line breaks, with no border; there is no monospace face in the system.
  5. Asap and Lucide sit in the list's alphabetical order like any library: "Asap and Asap Condensed (fonts)" with SIL OFL 1.1, "Lucide (icons)" with ISC, MIT.
