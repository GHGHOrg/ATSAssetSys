Choices made in this drawing, for you to confirm. Everything else follows D-30 (Cash's filters, chips and rows) and D-31.

  1. The portfolio is a read-only line at the top of the filter card ("Portfolio" in secondary 14, the name in medium 16), since there is no chooser.
  2. A trade's cash effect is a link row under it: indented, secondary 14 with its sign, uncolored, and a chevron; it opens the cash line. The original of a reversal gets "Reversed on …" the same way.
  3. A row's right side is the amount uncolored, or a split's ratio ("2 for 1") in its place; rows keep Cash's regular 16 title (not medium, as on Holding detail's Transactions tab).
  4. No chip is ever disabled here: every combination can match (marker 2), unlike Cash's (D-29).

Note for you: Holding detail's Transactions tab (D-43, 5) titles rows in medium 16; this list and Cash use regular 16. Both are accepted as drawn; if you want one rule, say which and I'll align the other.

Not drawn: the ticker list open, the date picker, 200% font. Icons are Lucide at stroke 2, drawn inline from its path data as Claude knows it.
