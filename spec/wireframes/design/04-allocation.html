Choices made in this drawing, for you to confirm. Everything else follows D-38 and D-39 (donut, rows, breakdown cards, the $0.00 line).

  1. The portfolio chooser is the standard pick field (56, D-31), labelled "Portfolio", at the top of the screen outside any card: it drives the whole screen, unlike Overall's dense "Allocation by" inside its card.
  2. The portfolio's value is the summary card with the 32 figure and the price age only; no cost or gain here (they are on the portfolio's screen).
  3. Without cash, the slices count from slice 1, so a holding keeps the color it has on Net Worth's By holding.
  4. A slice of 100.0% is a full ring with no gap, its percentage on the ring like any slice of 5% or more.

Not drawn: the chooser's list open (inside the window), updating and failed-refresh lines (as Overall), 200% font. Icons are Lucide at stroke 2, drawn inline from its path data as Claude knows it.
