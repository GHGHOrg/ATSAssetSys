Markers

  1. Date-range filter: From and To (the sale date, both ends included), each optional, and a Clear dates button. With both empty the view shows all dates (the default). The filter is not saved. The date note sits next to the inputs (section 6).
  2. Overall total of realized gain/loss in the range, with the number of sales. Green with a plus sign for a gain, red with a minus sign for a loss, no color at zero. Informational only (intent); no tax wording.
  3. By holding: one row per holding (a ticker within a portfolio) that has at least one sale in the range, in ticker order A to Z, each with its portfolio name, number of sales and total. Hidden holdings are included, because their sales still count. Tapping a row expands or collapses its sales.
  4. Format only: a loss, as OLDCO in the sample backup file (a sell with zero proceeds). It would also change the total, so the total in frame A is for Example A alone.
  5. Expanded holding: the arrow points up, and its sales are listed below, newest first.
  6. One sale: sale date with "ET", realized gain/loss, quantity, proceeds and cost of the lots used (4.4). Tapping it opens the sell in Transaction history of that portfolio, where its lots and any reversal link show. A sale cancelled by a reversal (4.6) is not listed and not counted, whatever the range.
  7. No sale in the range: the total shows $0.00 with no color, and one plain line replaces the list. The same applies when no sale exists at all.

Reached from More (Realized gains). The back arrow returns to More. Sales of a deleted portfolio are gone with it (4.7).
